1 · Home

Five blocks: full-viewport video hero with a very large title, one full-bleed video band, category tiles from clothing types, a "New outfits" carousel, one button. Then a one-line footer. Phone rule: only one video plays at a time (yellow "playing" tag; the rest show their poster).

2 · Product page

Large media (video first) beside the details. Description, Material, Delivery and Returns are collapsible, and their text is always in the page. "Similar outfits" shows the same clothing type. The sticky Add to cart bar stays on phone. Dev toggle (bottom right) compares the description font.

3 · Side menu, search and cart

The menu slides in from the left and is full height. It lists Shop, the clothing types, Account / Sign in, the language switcher (dimmed: flag off), and support or policy links only when supplied. The Theme row exists only if the client approves dark mode.

4 · Shop, sold-out card and empty search

5 · Dark variant (Pending client decision)

Brand colours only: Ink Black, Ivory, Emerald, and Red for the primary button. The Theme toggle in the top bar switches every frame; these are always dark. Contrast results are in the design notes.
