1 · Home

Full-bleed autoplaying, looping hero video with the brand name, introductory message and one Shop button; then a few large video-led outfits; then the page ends. Left: desktop. Right: 390 px phone.

2 · Shop, sold-out card and empty search

Large cards, three across on desktop, one on phone. A sold-out outfit is dimmed with a "Sold out" tag. A search with no match shows the exact message.

3 · Product page

The video is the hero. Size and Find My Size sit under the price. On a phone the Add to cart bar is fixed to the bottom. The description is real text in the page even when collapsed (use the dev toggle at the bottom right to compare Drafting Mono with Inter; no body font has been chosen).

4 · Navigation: menu, search overlay and cart

Phone header: Search icon, Cart, Menu button. The menu holds Shop, Sign in (Account when signed in) and the language switcher, shown dimmed because the Pidgin flag is off until approved copy exists. Desktop header: Shop, Search, Account, Cart.
