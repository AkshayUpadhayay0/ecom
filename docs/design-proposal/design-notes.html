Flags. (1) White on brand Red (#f5262d) is 4.04:1: it passes only as large text (18.66 px bold or more), so every primary button in this proposal is 19 px bold or larger. A button smaller than that would fail; the derived darker red already in the app (#e12329, 4.69:1) fixes it and needs client approval. (2) Emerald as text or as a border on Ink is 1.82:1 and fails: in dark mode Emerald is only ever a surface with Ivory text, and control borders switch to Ivory. (3) Red on Ink is 4.44:1: fine for graphics, not for small text. (4) Text on top of video cannot be computed from the palette; it relies on the scrim and must be checked on the real hero frames.

Home totals. First view on phone: about 50 to 80 KB of image plus the existing app JavaScript and two font weights (preloaded). Video bytes begin after load: about 0.9 MB for the hero, then about 0.5 MB when the band comes into view, then about 0.25 MB per card as each takes its turn, so a slow scroller on phone stays under about 2 MB and a full read about 3 to 4 MB. Desktop full scroll: about 6 MB, all from the CDN. Slow connection: posters only, no video requests, Play button, as the site does today.

Today's media pipeline makes one ~1280 px video poster and 480p/720p/1080p files. A phone-sized poster and portrait renditions are not built yet; they need an API change in the media pipeline.

Does the cinematic design put the mobile Lighthouse target (performance 85+, accessibility 100) at risk?

Yes, moderately, for performance. Accessibility 100 is not at risk if the rules below are kept.

  • LCP: a 184 px title and a full-viewport hero are fine as long as the poster is the LCP image, is small (phone rendition) and is preloaded. Never let the video be the LCP element. Needs the phone poster rendition.
  • Video weight: Lighthouse throttles to slow 4G. Do not start video before the load event and poster paint; hero video under about 1 MB on phone.
  • Total Blocking Time: parallax and reveal must not run heavy JavaScript. Use IntersectionObserver and transform/opacity only, or CSS scroll-driven animation. No animation library.
  • CLS: reserve every aspect ratio (4:5 cards, hero height, tiles) and use size-adjusted fallback fonts so the huge title does not jump when Drafting Mono loads.
  • Fonts: preload only the two weights above the fold.
  • Accessibility: every control keeps a name (icon buttons have labels), the carousel is a labelled, focusable region, the side menu traps focus and closes on Escape, and text over video keeps its scrim. axe cannot judge text over video, so it needs a manual check on real frames.

Content rules

  • Description, Material, Delivery and Returns are always real text in the page, even when collapsed (visual shortening, or a native details element that find-in-page opens).
  • No text is invented. [Bracketed] text is supplied by the client or Admin.
  • The footer About block is no longer shown. Its data stays; Admin → Content marks it "Not shown on the website".
  • No About page content, no newsletter.
