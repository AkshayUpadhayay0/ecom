  • Page gutters: 24 px desktop, 16 px phone (phone video cards run edge to edge).
  • Between sections: 72 px desktop, 48 px phone. Between cards: 48 px / 36 px.
  • Every tap target is at least 48 px high.

Colour usage (confirmed brand kit)

Ivory: page background
Ink: text, outlines, selected chips
Emerald: Find My Size, quiet accents
Red: the ONE primary button per screen
Marigold: graphics only, never text on light
Terracotta: large areas and graphics only
  • Small text uses the darker text variants already in the app (contrast 4.5:1 or better).
  • Text over video always sits on an Ink gradient scrim.
  • The ruler-tick motif is the only decoration. It uses Ink or Emerald at reduced opacity.

Motion rules

  • The hero video autoplays, muted, and loops. Everything else is quiet: a slow push on the hero, short fades (150 to 250 ms), no parallax, no scroll-jacking.
  • Reduced motion: no autoplay and no push. Posters show, with a play button as today.
  • Save-Data or a slow connection: posters only, no video download, as today.
  • Video budget: at most 4 videos play at once (MAX_CONCURRENT_VIDEOS); cards outside the screen never load video.
  • Overlays (menu, search, cart) open with a fade or slide of 200 ms, trap focus, and close with Escape.

Content rules

  • Description text is always in the page, as readable text, even when collapsed (visual shortening only, never removed from the page).
  • The footer About block is no longer shown on the site. Its data stays. The Admin Content page will mark it "Not shown on the website" instead of removing it.
  • No text is invented. [Bracketed] text is supplied by the client.
